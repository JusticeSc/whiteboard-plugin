# Historical-pattern distinctions

These calibrated contrasts explain how to judge a proposal; they are not a deletion inventory, package blacklist, or authority to restore source-product subsystems. Verify the current producer, consumer, and retained promise each time.

| Candidate evidence | Legitimate simplification | Counterexample that must remain |
|---|---|---|
| Vocabulary copied across layers with no producer or effective endpoint | Remove an unreachable distinction and its propagation after tracing every entry | A dynamic extension registry with no fixed in-repo caller can still have real third-party consumers |
| Renderer ignores a durable marker | Remove redundant display work when no observation contract changes | The marker can still prove publication, recovery, or first terminal outcome; renderer deadness does not prove data deadness |
| General schedule/transaction machinery costs more than the requested promise | Propose a smaller explicit schedule or nontransactional behavior and name the lost capability | Existing promised rollback, timezone, durability, or authorization behavior cannot disappear under a cleanup label |
| A validation looks duplicated | Reuse the authoritative enforcement at the operation boundary | Moving validation after the effect or letting alternate callers bypass it weakens the contract |
| Copied state can be derived | Read the authority at the required observation time and remove the duplicate lifecycle | Redaction, retention, snapshot timing, and successful-commit publication may differ despite equal values |
| A library appears to cover custom infrastructure | Compare complete maintained cost, including glue, tests, packaging, and transitive risk | A shim rebuilding the same framing to enforce limits may erase every apparent gain |
| A pure same-process value looks defensively copied | Borrow with an explicit retention/immutability contract | Process/wire/durable/model boundaries can transfer ownership or require validation; root freezing is not deep immutability |

For each candidate give the strongest keep argument, the observable retained guarantee, and a falsifiable test. Archived examples remain frozen; do not repair their links or treat their rejected alternatives as current policy.
